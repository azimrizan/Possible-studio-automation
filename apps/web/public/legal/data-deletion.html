Data Deletion

Possible Studio · Last updated 15 October 2026

You can remove your data from Possible Studio at any time.

1. Disconnect Facebook and Instagram

In the app, open Integrations and click Disconnect. This immediately deletes the Meta access token we store. You can also remove the app from your Facebook account: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, select Possible Studio and click Remove.

2. Request deletion of all your data

Email azimrizan7@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the Facebook name or Instagram account you connected. We will delete your account information, tokens, uploads, generated content and campaign records within 30 days and confirm by email.

Posts already published to Instagram and ads in your Meta ad account live in your Meta accounts; delete them there if you want them removed.
