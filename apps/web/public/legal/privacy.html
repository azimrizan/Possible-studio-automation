Privacy Policy

Possible Studio · Last updated 15 October 2026

Possible Studio ("we", "us") is an AI marketing tool that helps businesses create social media creatives, publish them to their own Instagram accounts and run ads in their own Meta ad accounts. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

Information we collect

  • Account information from Meta, when you choose to connect Facebook/Instagram: your name, the Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and ad accounts you grant us access to, and an access token issued by Meta.
  • Content you provide: product photos, logos, brand details, campaign goals, captions and other files you upload or type into the app.
  • Content we generate for you: posters, captions, ad copy and campaign plans.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the service, such as server logs.

How we use it

  • To create creatives and campaign plans you request.
  • To publish posts to Instagram and to create, pause or launch ads in Meta, only after you approve each action in the app.
  • To show you the status of your posts and campaigns.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your Meta data for advertising of our own.

Service providers

To provide the service, content you submit may be processed by: Meta Platforms (publishing and ads), Google Gemini (image generation), Groq (text and planning models) and, if enabled, a web search provider for market research. They process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and security

Meta access tokens are stored encrypted and are never shown in the browser. Uploaded and generated files are stored privately for your workspace.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your workspace is active. You can disconnect Facebook/Instagram at any time in the app (Integrations → Disconnect), which deletes the stored access token. To delete all of your data, see our Data Deletion instructions.
